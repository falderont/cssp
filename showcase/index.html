Product concept · v4 scope

The consolidated front end colocation providers don't have.

CSSP is a CRM + ITSM layer that sits on top of a data center's existing DCIM, CMMS and BMS — not a replacement for them — giving enterprise customers one login across every site they occupy, and giving the CS team one place to run the relationship.
